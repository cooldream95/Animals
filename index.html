Animals

Animals are multicellular, eukaryotic organisms that exhibit incredible diversity in form, function, and habitat, playing essential roles in ecosystems and the survival of life on Earth. Definition and Kingdom Animals belong to the kingdom Animalia. They are multicellular organisms with specialized tissues, capable of movement, consuming organic material for energy, and reproducing primarily through sexual means . Unlike plants and fungi, animals lack rigid cell walls, which allows mobility and complex organ development

Classification Of Animals

Classification The animal kingdom is broadly categorized into several major groups:
Mammals: Warm-blooded vertebrates with hair or fur; female mammals nurse their young with milk. Examples include elephants, whales, and humans

Birds: Warm-blooded, feathered vertebrates that lay eggs and are descendants of dinosaurs. They possess four-chambered hearts

Reptiles: Cold-blooded, scaly vertebrates, including snakes, lizards, turtles, and crocodiles

Amphibians: Cold-blooded animals requiring water for survival during early stages; includes frogs and salamanders

Fish: Aquatic vertebrates with gills and fins; includes sharks, ray-finned fish, and lampreys

Invertebrates: Animals without a vertebral column, including insects, spiders, snails, and octopuses; they constitute the majority of all animal species
